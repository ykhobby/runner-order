# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Plastic model and Gunpla builders entering manual references during a speed build, often at a brightly lit workbench with a desktop monitor or a phone beside their tools.

## Product Purpose

Turn freely named assembly sections and original parts-entry text into naturally sorted cutting lists by section or by runner. Save and reopen builds by name, and export fitted A4 cutting sheets. Work stays in the browser without accounts or external services.

## Brand Personality

Practical, calm, precise. The user confirmed a light, high-contrast layout, large controls, keyboard access, and no decorative motion.

## Anti-references

Commercial landing pages, decorative dashboards, preset anatomy categories, unnecessary animation, and features outside the requested cutting workflow.

## Design Principles

- Keep original entries available beside their organised result.
- Make errors visible without losing entered text.
- Keep section names and order entirely under the builder's control.
- Make the two cutting views a one-click choice.
- Preserve the current build before starting or opening another project.
- Default PDFs to one A4 page, with two or three pages available for more space.
- Prefer familiar controls and readable labels over decoration.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Semantic controls, labelled inputs, keyboard-operable section ordering, visible focus, readable contrast, and a narrow-screen layout. No motion is necessary.
